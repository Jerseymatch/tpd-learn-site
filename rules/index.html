Poker Rules: What Happens If…

Short answers to the situations that come up at a real poker table. Each one covers the rule, what you do as the dealer, and when you call the floor. As a dealer, you explain rules. Rulings are the floor's job.

Rules

One situation per page.

  • RuleWhat Happens When a Player Acts Out of Turn?When an out-of-turn action stands, when the player gets a new decision, and what the dealer does.
  • RuleWhat Happens When a Card Is Exposed?A flashed card on the deal, the blinds, a card off the table, a player flipping their own card, and tournaments.
  • RuleHow Does Running It Twice Work?Who has to agree, how to burn and lay out two boards, and splitting the pot between them.
  • RuleWhen Should a Dealer Call the Floor?Which situations go to the supervisor, how to call, what to say when they arrive, and what never to tell a player.
  • RuleIs Verbal Binding in Poker?Call, raise, pot and all in, words that contradict the chips, speaking after the chip lands, and when the dealer didn't hear it.
  • RuleShowdown DisputesOne card shown, a winner mucked, show one show all, slow rolls, heads-up flips and misreads: how the dealer handles each.
  • RuleDead Hands: What Kills a Poker Hand?Folds, the muck, too many cards, an absent player, cards landing on a hand, and the rules that don't kill a hand.
  • RulePoker All-In Rules: What Counts as All In?Saying it, pushing a stack, one-chip and oversized-chip all-ins, the all-in button, all-ins for less and tournaments.
  • RuleLive Poker Etiquette: Your First Time at a Casino Poker TableBuying in, asking the dealer, protecting your hand, clear checks and bets, tipping, phones and slow rolls.
  • RuleWhat Happens If the Dealer Turns a Card Too Early?The floor call, substantial action, the TDA reshuffle vs the old natural-river way, random card theory, and how to prevent it.
  • RuleHow Do Bad Beat Jackpots and High-Hand Promotions Work?The jackpot drop, qualifying hands, the split, verifying burns and muck, high-hand periods, and what the dealer does.
  • RuleHow Much Should You Tip a Poker Dealer?How much is normal, when to tip, losing hands, tournaments, how to hand it over, and where the tip goes.
  • RuleWhat Is the Poker TDA? Rules vs Recommended Procedures vs House RulesWhat the TDA is, rules vs recommended procedures, TDA vs WSOP vs house rules, the rules every dealer should know, and the TDA exam.
