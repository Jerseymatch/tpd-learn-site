Learn to Deal Poker: The Complete Guide

Everything it takes to deal poker professionally, start to finish — taught by a working dealer and trainer, built from real course footage and thousands of real corrections made to real students in live training classes. Not a rulebook summary. The actual mechanics, the actual judgment calls, and the actual mistakes almost everyone makes while learning them.

Work through the modules in order if you’re starting from zero, or jump straight to whatever you need to fix. Every lesson ends with the specific things students most commonly get wrong on that exact topic, pulled from real training-class correction logs — so you can skip straight past the mistakes that would otherwise cost you weeks of bad habits.
