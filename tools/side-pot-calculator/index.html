Side Pot Calculator

When two or more players go all-in for different amounts, the pot has to split into a main pot and one or more side pots — and getting the split wrong is one of the most common mistakes new dealers make (see mistake #4). Use this to check your math.

How side pots work

Every time a player is all-in for less than everyone else, their contribution caps a “layer” of the pot that only players who matched or exceeded that amount are eligible to win. Stack those layers from smallest all-in to largest, and you get the main pot plus however many side pots the hand needs. Folded players' chips still count toward the pot total — they just aren't eligible to win any layer.
