## Non-negotiable content rules
1. **Every claim comes from Marc's material**: lesson transcripts, class transcripts, his YouTube videos, or the correction log. If you can't find a source for a claim, cut it. No generic poker-dealer filler, and no invented stories, numbers or quotes.
2. **First-person Marc voice, bylined to him.** Plain, direct, practical, the way he talks in class. Every page starts with `"review": "pending"` in its meta until Marc reads it. Add it to the review list at the bottom of `docs/content-plan.md`.
3. **No student names on public pages. Ever.** Describe class moments anonymously ("a student", "nearly every student").
4. **Class footage and class corrections (LaCie / Vimeo) need a conversation with Marc first.** He's fine featuring corrections from some students and absolutely not from others, so never pick a class clip yourself: propose the specific clip and wait for his yes. Public YouTube videos can be embedded anywhere freely (Marc, Nov 16, 2026).
5. **No thin pages.** A page is published only when it's better than anything else ranking for that topic. Hubs show "coming next" cards (non-links) for planned pages, never stub pages.
6. **Affiliate products:** only items Marc actually recommends, from `src/data/gear.json` (the source is the "Materials Needed" lesson in the Complete Course on Ivorey). Use them where genuinely helpful. Never invent a recommendation. Links get `rel="sponsored nofollow"` and the inline disclosure, which the shortcode adds automatically.
8. **Protect a few sensitive things; use everything else (Marc, Nov 16, 2026).** Class material is a main source, so keep using it fully. But never publish: **how much Marc makes** (his hourly rate, tips or income, at any room), **what specific rooms pay**, or **private games he has dealt** (e.g. in Florida). If something else seems like it might not be a good idea to share (about other people, an employer, or anything personal or legally touchy), don't drop it quietly. Ask Marc: add it to "Needs Marc's OK" at the bottom of `docs/content-plan.md` and mention it in your summary. **Personal stories** (Marc's or a student's): think about the context they were shared in. A story told openly to make a teaching point is usually fine, anonymously for students. Something told in confidence, about a hard time, money, health, family or someone's job situation, needs Marc's OK first. **Use Marc's answer, not the student's situation.** Example: a student asked whether someone with a felony can be hired. Marc's explanation of how that works can go on the site. The fact that a student asked, or anything that hints at who or why, cannot. Turn it into a general question ("Can you become a poker dealer with a felony?") answered in Marc's words. If you aren't sure, ask. The goal is to protect those few things, never to lose good content.
9. **Dealers explain rules, never rulings (Marc, Nov 16, 2026).** A ruling is a decision the floor supervisor makes at the table. Never write that a dealer makes, explains or defends a ruling or a floor decision. A dealer can explain a *rule*; for a ruling, the dealer calls the floor and the supervisor explains it. Where Marc says rooms differ (e.g. some rooms have the dealer make the substantial action call), keep it: "it differs room to room" is accurate, not steering.
7. **truepokerdealer.com is off-limits.** It's GoHighLevel/Ivorey, where students log in to paid courses. Don't change it unless Marc has explicitly approved a specific plan.

## How to build a page (the pipeline)
0. **Find new pages (Marc's ask, Nov 16):** the goal is to turn everything Marc has ever taught into pages. At the start of each run, skim `tpd-knowledge/youtube/topics.md`. Any topic with lots of moments and no page (or no planned row) gets a new row under "Ideas from the YouTube index" in the content plan.
1. **Pick the next page** from `docs/content-plan.md`. Follow any PRIORITY note at the top.
2. **Gather sources.** Read the main lesson transcript in full, then search the correction log and class transcripts for the topic. Write a short source brief (claims → where they came from) in your scratch space.
3. **Draft** `src/pages/<section>/<slug>.html` following the template below.
4. **Independent fact check.** Spawn a separate agent that hasn't seen your draft process. Give it the draft and the source files, and have it list every claim that isn't supported by the sources, or that contradicts them. Fix or cut each one. This is required. The pages carry Marc's name.
5. **Build and check:** `python3 src/build.py && python3 scripts/check.py` (link, schema, shortcode and leftover-marker checks). Screenshot the page at 390px and 1280px wide and look at it.
6. **Publish:** commit to `main` and push (Cloudflare deploys in about 3 minutes). Confirm the page is live, update `docs/content-plan.md` (status + review list), and update hub "coming next" cards.

## Page template (see existing guides in `src/pages/deal/`)
- Meta JSON: `title` (≤ ~65 chars + " | True Poker Dealer"), `h1`, `crumb`, `description` (~150 chars), `dek`, `kicker`, `card`, `type: "article"`, `order`, `updated`, `published`, `hero_video`, `review`, `related`.
- Body: a 1–2 paragraph intro on why it matters → `[[video ID]]` (primary) → `[[toc]]` → H2 sections with steps → `<div class="classroom">` "What I correct in class" callouts from the correction log → `[[shorts ...]]`/`[[lite ...]]` where they help → practice section → `[[gear ... | heading]]` if relevant → `[[cta free]]` → "Common questions" H3 Q&As (real search phrasing, short direct answers) → `[[cta course]]` (or `school` / `call` on career and audition pages).
- Link between guides generously (procedure ↔ procedure, guide ↔ /gear/).
- **The free procedure manual** (`/procedure-manual/`, PDF in `downloads/`, noindex) is the site's email lead magnet. build.py automatically adds a "Get the free manual" box before "Common questions" on every guide. The form is Ivorey form `f7kFXtWyQSiKK6Suf1DN`; its workflow tags `procedure manual` and emails the link. Don't change that form or workflow without Marc.
- **The Remote School box** (sidebar on desktop, early inline mention on phones) is added automatically by build.py. Give each new page a `"rail_hook"` in its meta: one line tied to that page's topic, true to Marc's material (e.g. side pots: "Side pots are the one thing to get absolutely right before your audition."). Optional `"rail_course"` overrides the course line.
