A real poker room's dealer procedure manual, free

This is the manual a working poker room handed its dealers, with the room's details removed. It covers house rules, dealer procedures, the rake, fills, and opening and closing a table, exactly the way the room wrote them down.

Every room has its own version, and when you start working, yours is the one that counts. But reading a real one shows you exactly how seriously rooms take procedure, and what they'll expect from you on day one.

What's inside

Standards & policies

What the room expected from its dealers: the general poker room policies and the chain of command.

Rules & regulations

Hand rankings, hold'em, Omaha, stud and razz, the buy-in, table stakes, misdeals, all-ins, raises, string bets and more.

Dealer procedures

The shuffle and cut, calling for decisions, tracking and taking the rake, managing the rack and fills, and opening and closing a table.

Rake amounts and house policies in the manual are that room's own. Your room will have its own numbers and rules. For how I teach each procedure, start with the step-by-step guides.
